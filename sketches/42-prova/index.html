Sources and limits

  • Wind field: synthetic. A steady east wind with a little random wobble, made up for the starter template. It describes no real place or time.
  • Dot positions: synthetic, random at each page load.
  • No observed, reanalysed or modelled data is used yet.

What this sketch cannot show: any real weather. The wind never changes speed or direction, has no height, and is not tied to a map, so nothing here should be read as a measurement or a forecast.
